Executive Summary

System Architecture: The Unified Plant Theory presents a structured optimization framework that separates hydraulic and photosynthetic domains while unifying them through a profit maximization principle.

Critical Success Factors: Stomatal conductance remains the primary control mechanism. Accurate modeling of this trade-off is the key to improving the theory's explanatory power.

Optimization Landscape: Nonlinear hydraulic costs create a complex surface with possible local optima, which is where future strategy improvements are most likely to emerge.

Strategic Priority: Focus on stomatal control and nonlinear cost relationships first; those are the highest-impact intervention points in this graph.
